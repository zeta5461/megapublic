Proposed decision

This record proposes replacing decision 0001 and the subject-only rule in triage. The accepted historical decision remains unchanged. Desk acceptance of this replacement is still pending.

  1. Read the report body, resolve the tenant, and check its locale, form version and channel. Use causeOf and sameCause as routing context, with Intl.DateTimeFormat for locale order. Check body evidence before accepting their result; neither function establishes the mechanism of a timing anomaly.
  2. Merge only when the evidence supports the same cause. Different subjects can describe the same cause; identical subjects can describe different causes.
  3. Unknown tenant or unplaced cause: keep apart and investigate. Do not treat two unknowns as duplicates. Preserve report IDs and provenance when moving reports.
  4. Recompute ticket counts from unique report IDs. Preserve uncertain symptoms explicitly and revisit the classifier when investigation contradicts it.
  5. Route parser, form-hint and timing reports to their respective tickets. Preserve timing subcases in the same investigation and verify the mechanism and fixes before marking reports resolved.
